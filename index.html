About

Precision Below Ground — Reliability Above All

Waghela Enterprises delivers specialized tunnelling and utility infrastructure for main contractors across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region and Maharashtra. From Thane headquarters we focus on disciplined execution, site safety, and quality control — from crew induction through plant operations and finishing works.

  • Trenchless expertise — Pipe jacking & microtunnelling
  • Conventional methods — NATM, HDD, open trench
  • Site logistics — Coordinated plant, materials & manpower
  • HSE culture — Induction, GREEN CARD plant, PPE & QC

Capabilities

What we deliver

End-to-end capability for trenchless and open-cut infrastructure packages.

NATM

New Austrian Tunnelling Method for adaptable excavation in varied ground conditions.

Microtunnelling

Machine-driven microtunnelling for accurate alignment in constrained urban environments.

Pipe Jacking

Controlled jacking of pipes for utility corridors and crossings with minimal surface disruption.

HDD

Horizontal Directional Drilling for utility installations under roads, railways, and waterways.

Open Trench

Conventional open-cut excavation and reinstatement for pipelines and civil packages.

Logistics

Site logistics support — plant, materials movement, and coordinated crew deployment.
