About

Precision Below Ground — Reliability Above All

Waghela Enterprises is a specialised underground infrastructure contractor headquartered in Thane, Maharashtra. We deliver subsurface engineering across NATM tunnelling, microtunnelling, pipe jacking, HDD, and open-cut excavation — combining field experience with trenchless technology for complex projects in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

As a trusted subcontractor to Tier-1 EPC firms, our crews execute hard-rock tunnelling in congested urban corridors, below the water table, and in mixed ground — environments that demand technical expertise and precise field management.

  • Trenchless expertise — Pipe jacking & microtunnelling
  • Conventional methods — NATM, HDD, open trench
  • Site logistics — Plant, materials & manpower
  • HSE culture — Induction, GREEN CARD plant, PPE & QC

Capabilities

What we deliver

End-to-end capability for trenchless and open-cut infrastructure packages.

01

NATM

Sequential Excavation Method for complex tunnel geometries in rock and mixed ground — forepoling, SDA, lattice girders, wire mesh, and M30 shotcrete.

02

Microtunnelling

High-precision MTBMs for gravity sewers and highway/railway crossings, operating below the water table with line-and-grade accuracy.

03

Pipe Jacking

Hydraulic jacking from deep launch shafts for sewers and stormwater drains — adaptable across diameters and ground conditions.

04

HDD

Steerable trenchless solutions for river crossings, telecom ducts, and long-distance pressure pipelines with minimal surface restoration.

05

Open Trench

High-production open-cut with full shoring and dewatering for shallow utilities, foundations, and box culverts.

06

Logistics

Self-managed delivery of materials and large-diameter pipes to site, keeping project timelines on track.
